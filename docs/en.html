All features

AI stem separation

Demucs splits any song locally on your machine into vocals, drums, bass, guitar and piano. No cloud, no upload.

Multitrack studio

Cut, drag, fade and loop clips, with undo history, snapping and effects on a selected range.

Sing-along + auto-tune

Karaoke view with a note band and scoring. "Make it perfect" gently pulls your take onto the original melody.

Mastering

EQ, compressor and target loudness per EBU R128, with live A/B and 14 profiles from streaming to club.

Voice tools

Clone your own voices (locally or via ElevenLabs), text-to-speech tracks, per-clip voice swap.

3D spatial audio

Place tracks freely in space, as a 7.1.4 bed for headphones or an Atmos export.

THE STUDIO

Arrange like the pros, without the bloat.

Drop an MP3 and it becomes a track. Split it into stems, cut clips at the playhead, move parts between tracks, set fades with a single grab.

  • Tracks with volume, pan, mute, solo and a level meter in the track head
  • Clip effects: reverb, echo, vocal cleanup, normalize, reverse
  • Full undo history with jump-to-any-state
  • Autosave and its own project format: .audiola keeps everything in one file

SING-ALONG

Sing the lead, your version.

Audiola turns any song into your vocal booth: lyrics roll by, the note band shows the original melody, and you see live how well you hit it.

  • Punch-in: jump anywhere and re-sing from there
  • Count-in, latency calibration and mic picker with live level meter
  • "Make it perfect": auto-tune to the original melody, your voice stays yours
  • The take lands right in the studio as a new track

MASTERING

Loud, clean, done. On target.

Your mix runs through EQ, compressor and loudness normalization to exactly the LUFS you want. A/B switch for the honest comparison, waveform with click-to-seek.

  • 14 profiles: streaming, pop, rock, club, podcast … plus your own presets
  • BS.1770/EBU-R128 metering, so the result hits the target loudness exactly
  • Per track too: "Master track" opens the same panel as a dialog
  • Export as WAV, MP3 or AAC, with lyrics written as tags and .lrc

EQUALIZER & SPATIAL

Shape your sound, in 2D and 3D.

The 4-band EQ works on the studio mix live: drag the dots, listen, done. And when stereo isn't enough, place every track freely in space.

  • Live preview right on the studio mix, curve with the real frequency response
  • Spatial: 7.1.4 bed, drag sources around the circle
  • Both are saved in the project and applied on export

VOICES

Your voice, as many as you like.

Voice profiles come from a short recording, locally on your GPU or via ElevenLabs. Swap a clip's voice, generate speech tracks from text.

  • Mic picker with live level: you see at once that the right one is connected
  • Local models (CUDA/CPU) with Whisper transcription
  • Only your own or licensed voices

LIGHT & DARK

Your studio, your light.

Night shift in the dark studio or daylight by the window. Drag the handle.

SINGOLA

The karaoke stage for your party. Drop a song, hand out the mics, sing.
Lyrics and notes appear on their own.

🎤 Up to 4 players

Everyone with their own microphone and color. Pitch lines run live across the note band.

📝 Automatic lyrics

From tags, .lrc, Audiola projects or AI recognition. Once a song is recognised, the song memory keeps it.

🎯 Real scoring

Points for hitting the original melody, combos for streaks. Like back in the day, on your own PC.

🏆 Playlists & battles

Whole playlists or random picks, multiple rounds and standings, until a winner is crowned.
